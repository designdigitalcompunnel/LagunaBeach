CITY OF LAGUNA BEACH | TWO-PAGE WEBSITE DEMO

Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a browser. The other page is
building-permits.html. Keep the assets folder next to both HTML files.

DEMONSTRATION
Use the Building Permits quick link, the Permits dropdown, or search for
"How do I apply for a building permit?" to reach the inner page.

Additional search examples:
- How do I report a pothole?
- Where can I find the next Planning Commission agenda?
- Where can I find information about City recreation programs?

Search uses a small local index and opens sections within the demo.
Permit and newsletter features are previews. Opening the demo does not
submit permit applications, reports, or newsletter subscriptions.
The day/night and accessibility marks are decorative.

CONTENT AND MEDIA
Free Pexels footage of Laguna Beach by Logan Voss:
https://www.pexels.com/video/laguna-beach-california-27954147/

The homepage includes packaged AI-generated photos that illustrate City
services and community activities. Pexels stock photos are linked in
assets/laguna-config.js and have packaged image fallbacks. Illustrative
photos do not depict verified City sites or events.
Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/
City seal and the Building Permits hero photograph (Adobe Stock 793177600)
provided by the user. The skyline is illustrative.

The City Hall panel is a schematic locator map. Its directions link opens
Google Maps.

The homepage hero uses a solid background while the video loads or
when reduced-motion settings disable video. Each photo placement has its own packaged image.

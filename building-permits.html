Permit overview

Do you need a building permit?

A permit is generally required to build, alter, repair or demolish a structure. Both interior and exterior work may require a permit.

Check the City’s guidance for common exemptions. Contact the Building Division if you’re unsure whether your project needs a permit or planning review.

  • Residential workNew construction, additions and remodels.
  • Commercial workTenant improvements and changes to existing space.
